# First Wins — five things to ask [AI_NAME] in the first 24 hours

> **For the installing AI:** present this card at the very end of Part 1, right before the warm goodbye. Personalize it first — swap every bracketed example for something [PARTNER_NAME] actually mentioned during this install (the email they owe, the decision they're weighing, the thing they keep forgetting). Then save your personalized version to `~/[AI_NAME]/FIRST-WINS.md` so [PARTNER_NAME] can find it again tomorrow. Keep it to five. Scarcity is the point — this is a menu, not a manual.

---

[AI_NAME] is live. Don't sit there wondering what to ask it — steal one of these. Each takes under a minute and works right now, day one, no extra setup.

## 1. Make it write like you

Say: *"Draft a reply to this — [paste a real email or message you owe someone]."*

You'll get a reply in YOUR voice — the one [AI_NAME] just spent twenty minutes learning — not robot voice. Send it or tweak it. Either way, that email is off your plate.

## 2. Empty your head

Say: *"Here's everything on my plate right now: [braindump — messy is fine]. Organize it and tell me what I'm forgetting."*

A two-minute ramble comes back as a clean list, sorted by what's urgent — usually with one thing you'd genuinely forgotten at the bottom.

## 3. Text it from the couch

Tonight, from the couch, open Telegram and send a voice note: *"Draft a short message to [someone] about [the thing you keep putting off]. Don't send it, just show me."*

The draft comes back to your phone. That's [AI_NAME] working while you're nowhere near the keyboard. One thing to know: it answers while your Mac is on and awake. Asleep, your messages wait, and it catches up when you open the lid.

## 4. Borrow its brain

Say: *"I'm weighing [a real decision you're facing — option A vs option B]. Lay out the trade-offs and tell me what you'd pick."*

You'll get a straight recommendation with reasons — not a wishy-washy "it depends." Disagree with it? Say so. It argues back politely, and the thinking sharpens both of you.

## 5. Ask what it remembers

Tomorrow, open a new session in its folder and say: *"What do you remember about me? Show me the file."*

It'll tell you what it kept from today, and open the plain text file it lives in. Read it. Fix a line if it got something wrong. That's your AI's memory, and it's yours.

---

**One habit that decides whether any of this works:** start every session in the **[AI_NAME]** folder in your home folder. That's where its memory and instructions live. Start on the Desktop and you get a Claude that doesn't know you.
